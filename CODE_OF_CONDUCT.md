# Code of conduct

Pygg is built in the open by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone taking part in its issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good intent; disagree about ideas, not people;
- give and take criticism of work constructively, with evidence where there is some;
- keep to the topic, and keep private information private (yours and others', including keys, tokens and prompts);
- not harass, insult, threaten or discriminate against anyone, in public or in private.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the contact on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports are read by the maintainer only and handled confidentially.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
